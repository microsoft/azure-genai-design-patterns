# Workload readiness record

Copy this structure into the workload's approved private system. Do not commit a
completed workload record to this repository. Use synthetic examples only for a
public contribution.

Use references to approved private evidence rather than copying sensitive logs
or data. Explain not-applicable decisions. Record limitations and failed tests
alongside passing results.

## Decision

Record **proceed**, **rework**, or **stop**, the accountable decision maker, date,
accepted residual risks, and required follow-up. A production decision also needs
the operating owner, monitoring/support route, and recovery or disablement plan.

## Reusable learning

Record the generic lesson, the source asset owner to notify, and whether a
separate sanitized contribution is suitable for maintainer review under
[CONTRIBUTING.md](../../../../CONTRIBUTING.md). Readiness approval is not
approval to publish.
